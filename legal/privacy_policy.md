## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a premium educational experience, facilitate exam practice, and synchronize study progress, we collect certain personal and non-personal data.

### A. Personal Data Provided Directly by You
* **Account Registration Information:** When you create an account, we collect your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
* **Profile Management Data:** Any optional profile picture or customization selections you choose within the profile editor.
* **Support Interactions:** Information you provide when contacting customer support (e.g., payment references, screenshots of issues, and communication logs).

### B. Usage & Performance Data
* **CBT Test Records:** Subject selections, correct/wrong/skipped questions, scores achieved, time taken per exam, and test-taking history (used to compile your Performance Analytics dashboard).
* **eClassroom Progress:** Submissions for school assignments, study note views, and notifications fetched from center/sub-admin notices.
* **Bookmarks & Custom Study Material:** Questions you save or flag for later review.

### C. Device and Log Data (Collected Automatically)
When you use the Application, we may collect technical data transmitted by your device, including:
* Device Model and Operating System version (Android/iOS).
* Unique Device Identifiers (e.g., IP address, Firebase Installation ID).
* Application performance analytics (e.g., app crashes, navigation paths, and feature usage duration).

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business and educational purposes:
* **Account Provision & Sync:** To register your user account, authorize login sessions, and securely synchronize your study progress, history, and notes across multiple devices.
* **Performance Analysis:** To compute statistics on your practice tests and display insights into weak/strong subject areas.

## 4. Google Play Account and Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, we provide users with simple, transparent methods to delete their accounts and all associated data.

### How to Delete Your Account and Data:
1. **In-App Deletion:**
   * Open the Application, navigate to **Settings** (or tap the slide-out menu).
   * Tap on **My Account / Profile**.
   * Select **Delete Account**.
   * Confirm your deletion request.
2. **Web/Email Request:**
   * If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by emailing our support team at: **taiwoprints999@gmail.com** with the subject line "Request for Account Deletion". Please provide your registered email address.

### What Happens Upon Deletion:
* When you initiate account deletion, your user profile, registered name, email address, phone number, performance analytics, eClassroom logs, and purchase records will be permanently erased from our active Firebase databases within **7 business days**.
* Any backups or audit logs will be overwritten or securely anonymized. Once completed, this action is irreversible, and your premium subscription cannot be recovered.
